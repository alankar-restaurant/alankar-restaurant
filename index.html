About Us

Alankar Restaurant is located at Rz-5, Tuesday Market Road, Manas Kunj, Uttam Nagar, New Delhi. The restaurant is known for its delicious food, comfortable ambience, and excellent service.

We provide a great dining experience for families, friends, and food lovers. Guests can enjoy a variety of dishes along with special service options like All You Can Eat, Happy Hour Food, and a cozy fireplace environment.

Our goal is to deliver quality food and create memorable moments for everyone who visits Alankar Restaurant.
